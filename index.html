How it works

All year round

No single festival week. Films come through on a rolling basis, so there's usually something on the calendar.

Film nights

Selected screenings get a little more: wine and something to eat beforehand, a speaker or filmmaker afterward.

Tickets at the theater

Buy tickets straight from Mamaroneck Cinemas, like any other movie. The list just makes sure you hear first.

The venue

Mamaroneck Cinemas sits inside the old Playhouse, which opened on Mamaroneck Avenue in 1925 and came back to life as an eight-screen neighborhood theater in 2022. It's a good room for a film that deserves an audience.
